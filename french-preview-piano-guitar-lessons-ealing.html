Lessons shaped around you

APGL focuses on the person learning, not just a fixed book or syllabus. Lessons can combine music you enjoy with technical foundations and structured goals.

  • Piano or guitar, from foundational skills to more advanced repertoire.
  • Chords, rhythm, note-reading, technique and musical understanding.
  • Playing songs you enjoy while strengthening weaker areas.
  • Practical guidance for independent practice between lessons.

A clear view of progress

Progress matters more when students and parents can see it. Lesson goals and occasional written updates can highlight what has improved, what still needs attention and what to practise next.

That means learning at a sensible pace, with adjustments when a piece, technique or exam goal needs extra time.

Exam preparation, when it fits

For students interested in graded exams, lessons can include repertoire, scales, sight-reading, aural skills and mock performances. ABRSM or Trinity preparation can be discussed where appropriate.

An exam date should follow realistic preparation, not dictate it.

Home visits & online lessons

Based in Ealing, AntZ Music provides in-person piano and guitar lessons at students’ homes across Ealing and West London. Online lessons are also available for students further afield.

Get in touch to discuss your preferred lesson format, your location (for home visits), availability and current rates. Lessons are arranged individually.

Common questions

Are lessons suitable for beginners?

Yes. We can start with basic technique, rhythm and familiar music and build gradually.

Can lessons include music I choose?

Yes. Learning music you enjoy can be balanced with reading, technique and broader musical skills.

Do you prepare students for ABRSM or Trinity?

Exam preparation can be part of lessons when the student's level, goals and timescale make sense.

Where are lessons held?

In-person lessons take place at students’ homes in Ealing and surrounding parts of West London. Online piano and guitar lessons are also available.
