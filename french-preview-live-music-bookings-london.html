What kind of performance can you book?

The set can be discussed around the venue and audience, with acoustic pop and rock covers at its centre. Guitar, vocals and live looping can add energy and variety.

  • Pub and venue music nights.
  • Private parties, celebrations and smaller events.
  • Solo live sets and musician enquiries for collaborations or dep work.

A practical booking conversation

Useful details to send first are the location, date, start time, preferred length, type of audience and any venue sound requirements. That keeps the quote grounded in what your event actually needs.

For session work or a dep role, send the line-up, repertoire, rehearsal expectations and performance dates.

Common questions

Do you perform pop and rock covers?

Yes. Pop and rock are central to the live repertoire, alongside arrangements adapted for a solo setup.

Can you perform at a private party?

Private-party enquiries are welcome. Availability, duration and logistics will be agreed directly.

Is sound equipment included?

Equipment requirements depend on the venue and event. Mention what is provided so the setup can be agreed in advance.

How do I get a quote?

Send the date, location, set times and event details by WhatsApp or email.
