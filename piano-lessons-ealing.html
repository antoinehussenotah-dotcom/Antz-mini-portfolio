Piano lesson questions

Are piano lessons suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners start with posture, hand position, rhythm and reading, using music they enjoy so the first steps feel rewarding. Lessons then build gradually at a pace that suits you.

What age can my child start piano lessons?

I teach children, teenagers and adults. Young beginners, including children as young as four, start with short, playful lessons that build listening, rhythm and finger skills before moving on to reading music.

Do I need a piano at home?

For lessons at home you will need a piano or a keyboard to learn and practise on. If you are not sure what to get, ask me before you buy.

Do you prepare students for ABRSM or Trinity piano exams?

Yes, when the student’s level, goals and timescale suit it. Preparation covers pieces, scales, sight-reading, aural tests and mock performances, and the exam date follows realistic preparation.

Can I have piano lessons online?

Yes. If you live outside Ealing and West London, or simply prefer it, lessons can run online with the same personalised plan and WhatsApp support.

How much do piano lessons in Ealing cost?

Rates depend on lesson length and location. Message me on WhatsApp or email for current prices and availability.
