Booking questions

Do you perform at weddings?

Yes. I play weddings, from music for the ceremony or drinks reception to an evening party set. Tell me your timings and the feel you want and I’ll suggest what fits.

Do you bring your own sound equipment?

Yes. I bring a full PA system and all my own equipment. If your venue has its own sound system or volume limits, let me know when you enquire.

How far will you travel?

I’m based in Ealing and travel up to about an hour by car as standard, which covers London and much of the surrounding area. Longer journeys are possible for an extra travel cost.

How long do you play?

Bookings cover up to two hours of music as standard, arranged in sets around your event. Longer performances are available.

Can we request songs?

Yes. Send me your favourites, or a special song for a key moment, and I’ll let you know what I can play.

How much does it cost to hire you?

It depends on the date, how long you’d like me to play and how far I need to travel. Send me the details on WhatsApp or email and I’ll come back with a quote.
